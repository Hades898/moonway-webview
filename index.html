Who we are

Objects with history.
Rooms with soul.

Moonway is a design & production house for events and décor, working from El Gouna since 2018. We have dressed most of the hotels on this stretch of the Red Sea — weddings, festivals, winters, and worlds that stood for a single night.

We work the slow way: woven, carved, dried, knotted and painted by local artisans, from materials this coast already owns. Nothing arrives by the pallet. Everything carries the effort of the hands that made it — and our practice supports El Gouna’s Green Star Initiative for sustainable hospitality.

El Gouna · Every December

December is ours.

Every winter, most of El Gouna wakes up to a Moonway Christmas. Each hotel gets its own — and no two are alike.

Paper birds over laser-cut trees — a Moonway winter, by night.

Sailing Club · December

Cook’s Club · Beach Build

The beach, rebuilt.

An open-air gym carved from timber and rope — dumbbells turned by hand, a gorilla standing guard, and every kilo stamped with our seal.

The pumpkin amphitheatre — El Gouna

Rixos & Cook’s Club · October

October is ours too.

An amphitheatre of carved pumpkins, a witch cut from a single sheet, graveyards with hand-lettered tombstones. Playful, theatrical — and built by the same hands that knot the macramé.

Holi Color Festival — The Clubhouse, El Gouna

The Clubhouse · Holi

A festival
of colour.

The Clubhouse asked for Holi. We painted a Volkswagen bus, dressed a tuk-tuk in marigolds, raised a stage and draped the whole pool in ribbon — then let the colour do the rest.

Casino Night — Club Paradiso, El Gouna

Club Paradiso · After Dark

Card-suit garlands cut by hand, chandeliers over a working casino floor, feathers on every table. Proof that handmade doesn’t only mean daylight and linen.

Why it matters

Made by hand,
on purpose.

Macramé knotted over weeks. Centrepieces dried, not bought. Wood carved and paper cut in El Gouna workshops. Local, natural materials carry effort and history — and keep the craft, and the money, on this coast.
