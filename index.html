Who we are

A workshop,
not a warehouse.

Moonway is a design and production house for events and décor, working from El Gouna since 2018. We have dressed hotels the length of this coast — weddings, festivals, winters, and worlds that stood for a single night.

We work the slow way — woven, carved, dried and knotted by local artisans, from materials this coast already owns. Nothing arrives by the pallet.

Why it matters

Made by hand,
on purpose.

Macramé knotted over weeks. Centrepieces dried, not bought. Wood carved and paper cut in El Gouna workshops. Local, natural materials carry effort and history — and keep the craft, and the money, on this coast.
